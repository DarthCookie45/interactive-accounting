Hot Dog Van

Build your van’s business from its first investment through four weeks of accounts.

Run your van for four weeks

Create your van, record cash and credit transactions, and watch your live T-accounts update over a four-week month.

  • Monday–Saturday: complete each day’s entries, then start the next day.
  • Sunday: complete the trial balance, SPL and SFP, then view your company’s weekly results and start the next week.
  • Next week: assets, liabilities and capital carry forward; income and costs start afresh. Closing inventory becomes opening inventory.
  • Adjustments: any depreciation introduced in week one repeats using fixed weekly teaching rates. Recalculate receivables allowances when prompted.
  • Money: use two decimal places, including .00. Ignore VAT and loan interest.

Easy: transaction totals supplied; 100 points per correct answer plus streak bonuses.
Hard: calculate transaction totals; 200 points per correct answer plus streak bonuses, −100 for a wrong answer.

Both modes add 10 points per streak step. Hints halve the base points and streak bonus, preserving your streak without extending it. Mistakes reset your streak; you can retry. The final trial balance earns 250 points, or 125 with a hint.

Your selling price and adjustment rates stay fixed for all four weeks. Refreshing starts a new game. Explore achievements from your daily results.
