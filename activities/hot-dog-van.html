Hot Dog Van

Build your van’s business from the first investment to the week-end accounts.

Run your van for a week

Create your van, then work through seven days. Customer numbers and prices vary between runs. Choose the debit and credit accounts, enter both amounts to two decimal places, and check your entry. Correct entries post to the live T-accounts.

At each day’s results, complete the trial balance by dragging tiles or selecting a tile and its blank. Use the T-accounts and statements for reference. Earn 100 points for completing it, or 50 if you use its hint.

Easy: Sales and ingredient totals are supplied. Earn 100 points per correct entry plus 10 per streak step on a first-try answer. A hint halves the award and preserves your streak.

Hard: Calculate sales and ingredient totals from quantities and unit prices. Earn 200 points per correct entry, or 100 with a hint. Each incorrect submission costs 100 points. Scores can go below zero. Streaks are tracked for achievements.

Incorrect entries reset your streak in either mode. You can retry until the entry is correct.

Achievements: Post your first entry, finish a day without mistakes, reach a five-answer streak, sell 250 hot dogs, end the week in profit, or complete the week. Your final results show which you earned.

All amounts are in pounds. Ignore VAT and depreciation in this introductory scenario.
